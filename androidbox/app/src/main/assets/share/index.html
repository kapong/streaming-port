Not working?
  • A "not private" warning on the first visit is expected: the TV makes its own certificate. Choose Advanced, then Proceed.
  • iPhone and iPad: Screen Mirroring (Control Center) works best; Safari here needs iOS 27 or later.
  • Android phones: browsers can't share the screen (use Cast from the quick settings), but the camera works here.
  • Nothing on the TV after 20 seconds? Stop, and share again.
