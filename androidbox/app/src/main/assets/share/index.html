Share tothe TV

Connecting

Sharing from this page stops watching here, so the TV doesn't show itself.

Your screen or camera shows up on the TV next to the others. One way only: nothing comes back to you.

Anyone on this network can watch this TV, including what you share. Don't share anything private.

On the TV

Keep this page open while you share. Closing it stops sharing.

Not working?
  • A "not private" warning on the first visit is expected: the TV makes its own certificate. Choose Advanced, then Proceed.
  • iPhone and iPad: Screen Mirroring (Control Center) works best; Safari here needs iOS 27 or later.
  • Android phones: browsers can't share the screen (use Cast from the quick settings), but the camera works here.
  • Nothing on the TV after 20 seconds? Stop, and share again.
  • Watching: keep this device on the TV's Wi-Fi. "TV busy" means every viewer place is taken; the page tries again by itself.
