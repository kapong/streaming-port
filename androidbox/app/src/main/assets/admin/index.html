    Miracast

    For Android phones and Windows laptops. Chromecast is always on and needs nothing here. While Miracast is open the TV leaves the Wi-Fi: AirPlay, Chromecast and this page stop working until someone presses BACK on the remote, and the TV may need its Wi-Fi reconnected in Settings > Network.

    Limits

    Largest picture a sender may send: 720p is 1280×720 (phones up to 720×1280), 1080p is 1920×1080 (phones up to 1080×1920), 1440p is 2560×1440, 4K is 3840×2160. 1440p and 4K allow up to 4 screens. The box decodes four 720p screens smoothly, but only about three 1080p screens or one 4K screen; past that, moving pictures stutter (phones never send 4K; 4K Macs can). The TV shows everything at 1080p. Changes apply to senders who connect next; nobody already mirroring is cut off.
